# Risk of Bias

## Workflow

1. Confirm that appraisal is required and select the current official tool
   appropriate to each design; record any justified adaptation.
2. Freeze study or result targets before inspecting effect direction.
3. Pilot the instructions on varied targets and correct tool-application errors.
4. Have one AI answer signalling questions and propose domain judgments with
   concise Tier 2 evidence.
5. Have an isolated second AI check overall judgments, critical domains, and
   low-confidence or internally inconsistent answers.
6. Route material judgment disagreements, missing information, conflicting
   reports, tool ambiguity, and overrides to humans. Do not restart unaffected
   assessments.
7. Audit a reproducible varied sample and reconcile every target.

Do not convert judgments to an unvalidated numerical quality score or exclude a
study automatically because of appraisal.

When serializing shared artifacts, read [the contracts](../systematic-review/references/contracts.md); do not invent field names.

Finish `READY`; the next phase is synthesis planning.
