# Reporting Package

## Workflow

1. Select the applicable reporting guideline and journal requirements.
2. Before drafting public prose, identify internal audit and control material.
   Exclude internal phase/status language, control IDs, audit terminology, and
   AI workflow details from the manuscript except where a truthful disclosure is
   required.
3. Before full drafting, obtain approval for a concise reporting blueprint that
   defines each section's purpose and a finding-led Discussion structure. Choose
   either neutral academic style or author-calibrated style; test a short sample
   first when using the author's voice.
4. Maintain a claim ledger only for published numbers, primary outcomes,
   certainty statements, protocol deviations, major limitations, and conclusions.
5. Draft Methods and Results from internal review artifacts. Keep external
   contextual literature clearly separate.
6. Draft Introduction, Discussion, limitations, conclusion, Abstract, and title
   without overstating design, certainty, applicability, or causality.
7. If using a general prose or humanization workflow, lock counts, citations,
   scientific terminology, causal qualifiers, negation, eligibility wording,
   and other meaning-bearing text before revision and verify them afterward.
8. Reconcile all PRISMA counts, tables, figures, supplements, identifiers,
   declarations, funding, conflicts, authorship, registration, and AI disclosure.
9. Send the complete candidate package to independent QC. Resolve findings
   locally, regenerate only affected outputs, then prepare the final M5 subject.

Formatting changes after scientific reconciliation do not require a new
scientific approval unless they alter meaning or data.

Call a reconciled draft technically validated or author-review ready, never
author approved or complete, until the required human approval is recorded.

When serializing shared artifacts, read [the contracts](../systematic-review/references/contracts.md); do not invent field names.

Finish `APPROVED` only when QC is clear and M5 human approval binds the final
publication package.
