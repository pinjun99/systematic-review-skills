# method modules

Read only the module or modules activated by the approved protocol.

## Clinical intervention

Use population, intervention, comparator, outcomes, timing, setting, and study
design. Prefer current official design-appropriate appraisal tools. Prespecify
effect measures, time points, and pooling rules.

## Observational or association

Define exposure, comparator or reference, outcome, population, timing, and
eligible designs. Extract confounder handling and adjusted estimates. Do not
force intervention-review assumptions.

## AI diagnostic, prediction, or evaluation

Define intended use, population, index model, target or reference standard,
setting, validation type, and eligible performance outcomes. Distinguish model
development from internal and external validation.

## Preclinical and organ bath

Define species, tissue or preparation, experimental conditions, exposure or
intervention, comparator, outcome, and design. Preserve concentration units,
normalization, bath conditions, controls, and repeated-measure structure. Do
not require human-clinical appraisal or GRADE without justification.

## Scoping and evidence mapping

Define population, concept, context, evidence-source types, and mapping
dimensions. Critical appraisal and certainty are optional unless required by
the review objective.

## Multiple modules

Activate more than one module only when the question genuinely spans them.
Assign every module-specific field and synthesis group to an owning module.
