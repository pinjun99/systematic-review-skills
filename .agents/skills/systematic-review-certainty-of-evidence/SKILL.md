# Certainty of Evidence

## Workflow

1. Confirm applicability and the prespecified important outcomes.
2. Use the current appropriate certainty framework and starting level for each
   body of evidence.
3. Judge every required domain with concise Tier 2 support from synthesis,
   appraisal, and study data.
4. Distinguish no evidence from very-low-certainty evidence.
5. Route borderline rating decisions, mixed designs, indirectness disputes,
   suspected publication bias, and upgrading to humans.
6. Reconcile certainty labels, explanations, and Summary-of-Findings values with
   the synthesis.

Do not make recommendations unless the review scope explicitly includes a
separate recommendation process.

When serializing shared artifacts, read [the contracts](../systematic-review/references/contracts.md); do not invent field names.

Finish `READY`; the next phase is reporting.
